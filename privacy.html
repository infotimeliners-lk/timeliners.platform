Overview

TimeLiners is committed to keeping your data safe. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with when you use timeliners.lk. By using the platform, you agree to these practices.

What We Collect

1. What you give us directly

  • Name, email, and specialty when you register, or your Google account name, email, and profile picture if you sign in with Google
  • Profile information: city, bio, experience, skills, social links, and any contact details you choose to add, such as a WhatsApp number, email, or phone number
  • Portfolio content: project videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and tags
  • Messages you send us via email or WhatsApp
  • Payment details and confirmations you send us when you buy TimeLiners Pro, for example over WhatsApp

2. Collected automatically

  • Profile and project view counts
  • Hire Me button click counts
  • Basic analytics events used to show you your own stats
  • Your IP address, used to prevent abuse such as inflated view and click counts
  • Basic device information, used to recognise trusted devices when you log in

How We Use It

  • To create and manage your account and portfolio
  • To display your public portfolio to visitors
  • To send transactional emails, such as account notifications and product updates
  • To show you your own analytics on the dashboard
  • To provide support via email or WhatsApp
  • To arrange and confirm TimeLiners Pro purchases and activate Pro features on your account
  • To detect abuse, block bots, and protect the platform

Who We Share It With

We do not sell or trade your data. We share it only with the services that run the platform:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and account/profile data. Your data lives here.
  • Cloudflare R2: stores your uploaded portfolio videos, thumbnails, and images.
  • Netlify: hosts the platform.
  • Google: if you choose to sign in with Google, Google confirms your identity and shares your name, email, and profile picture with us.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection on sign up. It may check your IP address and browser signals to tell people from bots.
  • Resend: sends transactional emails on our behalf.
  • Legal: we may disclose data if required by Sri Lankan law or a valid legal process.

Your Public Portfolio

Everything on your public profile, including your name, bio, skills, services, and published projects, is visible to anyone with your portfolio link. You control what is published. Projects can be set to draft at any time from your Dashboard.

If you add a WhatsApp number, email, or phone number to your profile, visitors can see it when they tap Hire Me. Only add contact details you are happy to share publicly. TimeLiners is not part of any conversation that follows.

Data Security

Your data is stored on Supabase with industry standard encryption and Row Level Security, meaning users can only ever access their own data. No system is 100% secure, but we take all reasonable measures to protect yours.

Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, projects, and associated files are removed from our primary systems. We are not responsible for copies that may persist in backups or with third party service providers beyond our reasonable control, except where retention is required for legal or financial records.

Cookies and Browser Storage

TimeLiners uses only essential cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember trusted devices, and keep the platform fast. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a revised date. Continued use of the platform after changes means you accept them.
