About TimeLiners
Sri Lanka's platform for hiring video editors & motion designers.
TimeLiners was built because Sri Lankan clients had no easy way to find a video editor they could trust, and editors had no platform that understood the craft, the hustle, or the local context. So I built one: a place where clients can browse real work and hire directly, and editors can build a portfolio, get discovered, and own their brand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hiring editors, building a portfolio, and how TimeLiners works.

Hiring an Editor
Is TimeLiners the best place to hire a video editor in Sri Lanka?
TimeLiners is built specifically for finding and hiring Sri Lankan video editors and motion designers. Every profile is backed by real, playable work, not just a resume, so you can judge editing quality before you ever reach out.
How do I hire an editor?
Browse Discover, open any portfolio you like, and tap "Hire Me" to contact them directly through WhatsApp, email or phone, whichever they've set up. There's no middleman and no TimeLiners commission, you deal with the editor directly.
Can I filter editors by style or niche?
Yes. Use the category filter on Discover to narrow down to Long Form, Shorts & Reels, Motion, Film and more, or search by keyword. Every project is tagged so you can zero in on the exact skill set you need.
Is it free to hire someone?
Yes. TimeLiners never charges hirers and never takes a cut of any project. You agree on price, scope and timeline directly with the editor.
Do I need an account to hire someone?
No. You can browse portfolios and use the Hire Me button without signing up. An account is only needed if you're an editor building a portfolio.
Building Your Portfolio
What is TimeLiners?
TimeLiners is Sri Lanka's platform for hiring video editors and motion designers, and the place editors go to get discovered. Build a professional portfolio, upload your projects, and share your profile link anywhere: Instagram bio, WhatsApp, client emails. Your portfolio becomes your professional identity, and the way clients find and hire you.
Is TimeLiners free?
Yes, TimeLiners has a free plan and an optional Pro Lifetime upgrade. Every account gets a public portfolio page, unlimited projects, 1 GB of video storage, social links, and a Hire Me button at no cost, no credit card needed. Pro Lifetime is a one time LKR 7,990 payment that helps more clients discover you with a feed boost and a verified badge, and adds an availability status, custom fonts and colors, and removes TimeLiners branding. See the Pricing page for the full breakdown.
How do I create an account?
Click "Join TimeLiners" in the top navigation bar, then switch to the "Sign Up" tab. Fill in your name, email, specialty and a password · your account is created instantly.
How do I upload a project?
Log in, go to your Dashboard and click "Upload Project". Fill in the project title, category, description, and tags, then upload your video files (MP4 only, up to 1 GB total storage) and a cover image. Your project goes live on your portfolio immediately.
How many projects can I upload?
As many as you like. There's no project limit. Your only constraint is total video storage, which is 1 GB per account.
How do I get found by clients?
Tag your projects accurately, use a clear cover image, and pick the right category for each upload. Clients filter and search by these. A complete profile with your best 4 to 6 projects converts far better than a large but unsorted one.
Can clients contact me directly?
Yes. Every editor profile has a Hire Me button that lets clients contact you directly. If you add a WhatsApp number to your profile, a WhatsApp contact button appears too. TimeLiners is not involved in the conversation after that.
Can I update my portfolio after publishing?
Yes. You can upload new projects, edit your profile details, change your cover image, and update your social links at any time from the Dashboard · no need to contact us.
TimeLiners Pro
What is TimeLiners Pro?
Pro is an optional upgrade that helps more clients discover you. Pro profiles rank higher in the feed and get a verified badge next to their name, so your work gets seen first. It also unlocks an availability status (Open to Work, Full time, Part time, or Not Available), custom main and sub fonts, a custom accent color, and the option to hide the "Made with TimeLiners" branding from your profile.
Is Pro a subscription?
No. Pro is a single LKR 7,990 payment made once. There's nothing to renew and nothing expires. Once you're on Pro, you keep every Pro feature for as long as your account exists.
How do I get TimeLiners Pro?
Go to the Pricing page and tap "Get Started" on the Pro card, or message us on WhatsApp directly to arrange payment. Once confirmed, we activate Pro on your account, usually within a few hours.
Account
Can I sign back in with my password?
Yes. Your account is securely stored and you can sign out and sign back in from any browser using your email and password. Your profile, projects and settings are all saved and will be there when you return.
